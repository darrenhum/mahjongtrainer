Our table rules

Hong Kong Old Style has house-rule variations. This trainer uses the following learning rules, not a universal payout calculator.

Chicken hand
0 fan — not enough to win here
All pungs
3 fan
Half flush (one suit + honors)
3 fan
Full flush (one suit, no honors)
7 fan
Each dragon pung
1 fan
Seat wind pung
1 fan
Round wind pung
1 fan

Minimum: 3 fan. Fan are scoring units, not cash points. Payouts vary by table and are not calculated here. A pung is three identical tiles; a chow is three consecutive numbers in one suit. A standard winning hand has four sets and a pair.

All pungs can combine with a flush and value sets. Half flush and full flush never stack. A wind pung earns 2 fan if it is both your seat wind and the round wind. A pair of dragons or winds earns no set fan.

Flowers, bonus win conditions, kongs, special limit hands, and payment rules are outside this first lesson set. No flower or win-method bonuses are assumed. Scenarios are guided choices under stated assumptions, not guaranteed optimal play or a complete defensive strategy.
